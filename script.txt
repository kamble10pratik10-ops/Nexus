[PROBLEM STATEMENT — 0:30–1:10]
Speaker 2:

"Today's supervisory model is broken in three specific ways.

First — Self-Attestation. SOCs evaluate their own performance and submit
checklists. There is no independent verification of whether the evidence
supports the claims.

Second — Invisibility of Failure. A SOC can silently close critical cases in
under two minutes, generate copy-pasted investigation notes, or allow
high-value assets to go completely unmonitored — and none of this appears in
any current compliance report.

Third — Scale Impossibility. SEBI's supervisory team cannot manually audit
thousands of alerts and cases across dozens of entities. Without intelligent
triage, human oversight is statistically meaningless.

The result? Regulated entities that look compliant on paper but are
operationally blind on the ground."

"We built NEXUS — SAT-SA Nexus — a Supervisory Analytics platform that
converts raw SOC operational data into evidence-backed supervisory signals.

NEXUS does not replace the SOC. It sits above it.

It independently evaluates whether the evidence produced by a SOC actually
demonstrates the capabilities it claims to have.

Think of it as an external auditor that never sleeps, never gets fooled by a
clean-looking dashboard, and never relies on a self-scored checklist."

--- QUICK Q&A PREP NOTES (not part of the script) ---

Q: Why not use an LLM like ChatGPT or Gemini for the NLP?
A: Because SOC telemetry is classified. No sensitive operational data can
   leave a regulator's environment. Our entire AI stack runs air-gapped and
   locally. That's a hard requirement, not a tradeoff.

Q: What prevents a SOC from gaming NEXUS by cleaning up their data before
   submission?
A: Two things. First, cryptographic hashing of the submission manifest makes
   post-hoc tampering detectable. Second, NEXUS explicitly looks for
   'too-clean' data — perfectly round MTTR numbers, absence of any anomalies,
   suspiciously uniform severity distributions — these are themselves red flags.

Q: How is this different from a SIEM or SOAR?
A: A SIEM detects threats in real time. NEXUS evaluates whether the SOC that
   runs the SIEM is doing its job correctly. We assess the assessor. These are
   completely different and complementary layers.

Q: Is this production-ready?
A: The backend is built on FastAPI + PostgreSQL, fully containerized with
   Docker. The benchmark harness is integrated. We are production-architecture
   ready and submission-ready for pilot deployment with a regulatory body.
