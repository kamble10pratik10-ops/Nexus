# SAT-SA Nexus: Evidence-Driven Supervisory Assurance Platform

## Tagline
From reported capability to demonstrated capability.

## Core Proposition
SAT-SA Nexus is an offline supervisory assurance platform that converts periodic SOC submissions into evidence-backed assessments of what a SOC has demonstrated, what remains unverified, what contradicts its declared capabilities, and where limited human supervisory review should be spent.

**Market Positioning:** We are not replacing the SOC stack (SIEM, SOAR, UEBA, etc.). We independently assess the evidence produced by the SOC stack. The system sits above SOCs to determine whether the SOC's operational evidence demonstrates that the SOC is actually functioning effectively. This addresses regulatory demands (e.g., SEBI's CSCRF, NIST frameworks) that require measurable, evidence-backed efficacy reports rather than self-scored checklists.

## Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)
1. **Evidence-Driven Efficacy:** Moves beyond checklist compliance by strictly measuring *demonstrated* capabilities against *declared* capabilities using statistical and NLP-driven anomaly detection.
2. **Air-Gapped / Privacy-First AI:** 100% offline and local NLP. Uses lightweight HuggingFace transformers (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`) and local ML algorithms (DBSCAN, Isolation Forest) without relying on any external, cloud-based LLM APIs (No OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini). Ensures absolute data privacy for sensitive SOC telemetry.
3. **Multi-Seed Scientific Validation:** The platform includes an integrated benchmarking harness (`benchmark_harness.py`) that uses deterministic synthetic fault injection to scientifically prove and measure the exact precision, recall, and False Positive Rate of its own detection engines.
4. **Metric & Automation Integrity:** Detects silent operational decay, such as SOAR auto-closure defects, severity downgrades to meet SLAs, and "zombie cases" (cases closed instantly without required investigation actions).
5. **Cryptographic Provenance:** Generates a signed submission manifest using offline HMAC-SHA256 signing keys to guarantee chain-of-custody for audit trails.

## Architecture & Key Principles
- **Batch-First:** Designed for periodic submissions. Explicitly excludes continuous collection, real-time monitoring, and SIEM functionality.
- **Three-State Assessment:** Findings evaluate claims as **SUPPORTED**, **CONTRADICTED**, or **UNVERIFIABLE**. We state what the evidence substantiates.
- **Hybrid Analysis Architecture:** 
  - **Deterministic (Pandas):** Fast closure rules, SLA resets, Zombie cases.
  - **Statistical (Robust Z-score):** Anomaly thresholds for peer deviations (e.g., MTTR deviations).
  - **NLP (Sentence-Transformers + DBSCAN):** Identifies semantic templating and repeated, low-effort SOC investigation notes disguised as unique text.
- **Explainability (Why Selected?):** Every finding explains WHAT happened, WHY it is unusual, and WHAT evidence supports it.

## Execution Roadmap (Ruthless Prioritization)

**P0 - Non-negotiable (Scientific Validation & Integrity)**
- Multi-seed benchmark validation (Proving high precision/recall over multiple random generator runs).
- Hard-negative dataset and varied honest-analyst text generation to eliminate NLP false positives.
- Signed submission manifest (Proves submission integrity).

**P1 - Core Product Differentiation (The Pitch)**
- Declared vs Demonstrated (Claims Verification Matrix).
- Detection Capability Drift (Is the SOC silently degrading?).
- Validated Coverage (Asset -> Log Source -> Rule -> Technique -> Observed/Tested).
- Adaptive Supervisory Sampling (The intelligent review queue).

**P2 - Domain & Expansion**
- Remediation effectiveness (Does the same root cause recur?).
- Peer taxonomy normalization (Mapping disparate SIEM alerts to MITRE ATT&CK).
- Automation assurance (AI/SOAR vs human accuracy).

## North Star Metric
*Supervisory Assurance Coverage* — Measuring Review Yield (confirmed findings / reviewed cases), Search-Space Reduction (1 - selected_cases / total_cases), and Review Efficiency (confirmed findings / review minutes) to prove the platform's ability to radically scale evidence-backed oversight without linearly scaling human auditors.
